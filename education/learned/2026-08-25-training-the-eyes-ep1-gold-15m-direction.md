# Training The Eyes Ep. 1 — gold futures 15m, the series opener

The first episode of the series, and the one that states what the series is FOR: *"direction bias
seems to be people's biggest problem."* He walks one gold chart left-to-right on the 15-minute and
narrates every level as it forms. No entry model taught — this is bias only.

🔴 **This note exists because Ep. 1 was being QUOTED in this repo without having been watched, and
the quote was wrong.** See *The misattribution* below.

## What it covers

**[00:56–01:31] The primitive, immediately.** A bullish move takes out highs → buyers induced →
they buy the reactions → *"as soon as we move away, we have liquidity below these lows."* Marked
with a horizontal ray and named as a **future target**.

**[02:05–02:25] Counting the touches.** He drags a box over a level and counts price using it as
support *"once, twice, three times, and four times"* before it finally moves away — more touches,
more liquidity. Same touch-counting as Eps. 5 and 9.

**[03:18–04:03] 🔴 A high with NO liquidity above it, stated plainly.** *"We had this buildup and
then we ran that buildup. Created this high with no liquidity above it."* This is the "no liq"
object — created by a sweep, never returned to. He then marks the internal liquidity below it as
the sellers' position.

**[04:43–05:00] The rule for when a level will NOT be run.** *"This high here should not get ran.
The reason being we stabbed liquidity out, created this high and then moved away. So there
shouldn't be any liquidity above this high yet — unless we come in and tap it."* An unswept high is
only a target once price returns and respects it.

**[05:00–05:31] 🔴 DO NOT WAIT FOR THE EXTREME.**

> *"Sometimes they'll still want more of an extreme entry and they'll wait for price to come all
> the way to the extreme. It's not necessary. **You will find a lot more consistency if you just
> take entries right when the liquidity is taken.**"*

Stop: *"always above that high to the left."*

**[05:31–06:26] Management.** Target the opposing lows. *"As soon as we start running these internal
points, we could definitely look to partial or roll our stop loss."* Then he does it: *"now that
we've taken out this internal high, you want to be either paying yourself and/or rolling your stop
loss. So in this example, we're going to roll our stop loss above that high."*

## 🔴 The misattribution — Ep. 1 does NOT say what this repo said it said

[[2026-08-13-10000-hours-of-liquidity-htf-only]] cites *"the four separate no-rolling warnings
(Training The Eyes Eps. 1, 7, 9, 10)"* as evidence that Ep. 1 warns against moving the stop.

**It does not. Ep. 1 does the opposite — it moves the stop, on camera, and tells you to.** At
[06:08] he rolls the stop above a high the moment an internal level is taken.

⚠ **The conclusion in that note still stands and is arguably stronger** — the reconciled rule is
"move the stop when a LEVEL is taken, never on a favourable excursion", and Ep. 1 is a clean
example of the first half rather than a counter-example. But the citation was made from a
reference in another video, not from watching this one, and it was wrong about which half.

**Standing lesson, and it is the repo's own rule 7 in a different costume: a citation is a CLAIM
about a source somewhere else. Watch the source, or do not cite it.** Fix the line in that note
before anyone builds on the count of four.
