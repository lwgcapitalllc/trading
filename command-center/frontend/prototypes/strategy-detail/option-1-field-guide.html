London session breakout

breakout MetaTrader 5 · forex

During the quiet Asian session the strategy measures the day's reference range. When London opens it watches for price to break out of that range, enters in the breakout direction, and aims for a multiple of the risk it took — flattening everything before the session ends. The edge: only trade days where the overnight range is neither too dead nor too wild, and let London's volatility expansion do the work.
