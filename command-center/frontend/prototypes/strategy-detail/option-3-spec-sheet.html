London session breakout

breakoutMetaTrader 5 · forex

What it does

Measures the day's reference range during the quiet Asian session, then trades the breakout when London opens — targeting a multiple of risk and force-flatting before the session ends.

Where the edge is

Only trade days whose overnight range is neither dead nor already-spent, and let London's volatility expansion carry the move. The Range filter and Profit target are where most tuning lives.
