London session breakout

TypeBreakout Runs onMetaTrader 5 MarketForex Parameters17 · 4 essential

What it does

During the quiet Asian session the strategy measures the day's reference range. When London opens it watches for price to break out of that range, enters in the breakout direction, and aims for a multiple of the risk it took — flattening everything before the session ends.

01 · ASIAN
Measure the range
00:00–06:00 GMT high-low sets the day's reference band.
→
02 · FILTER
Is the day tradable?
Skip days whose range is too small (no fuel) or too large (already moved).
→
03 · LONDON
Trade the break
Enter on the breakout, target a multiple of risk, force-flat before close.

The edge

Only trade days where the overnight range is neither too dead nor too wild, and let London's volatility expansion do the work. The Range filter decides which days qualify and the Profit target sets how hard each winner is pushed — that's where most of the tuning lives.
